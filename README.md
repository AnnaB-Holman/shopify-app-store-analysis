# Analyst Memo - Shopify App Store Insights

## Dashboard: Shopify App Store Analysis

## Reporting Period: Latest available data

## Key Insight
Review Rating numbers increased by 2x in 2022, but the Rating score dropped 0.5% from 4.33 to 4.12. This trend has continued through the first half of 2023, with the Rating score dropping another 0.1% to 4.08.

## Business Impact
Reviews and Ratings apps had the highest number of reviews at 184, 21% higher than with SEO apps, at 144. However, SEO apps have a  0.3% lower Rating score of 4.10, compared to Reviews and Ratings apps at 4.22.

## Recommendation
Recommend further analyzing if there is a correlation between Developer Reply Percentage dropping and Rating score dropping.
